# Voice & Tone

Porsche writes human, on eye level. Our cars are built for people — so we communicate the same way. Every interaction, whether product UI, email or error message, should be immediately recognizable as Porsche.

---

## The Four Principles

Four principles define how Porsche writes. Three are **Fundamentals** — the baseline for *everything* we say (products, services, articles, pure information). One is **Extra** — reserved for special moments.

| | Principle | In one line | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Concise** | Write to be understood. Show confidence in clarity. | Fundamental |
| 2 | **Authentic** | Speak like a person, not a corporation. Connect on eye level. | Fundamental |
| 3 | **Refined** | Combine finesse and intelligence. Articulate an unmistakable attitude. | Fundamental |
| 4 | **Inspiring** | Paint pictures in people's minds. Make dreams become reality. | Extra |

> **Fundamentals vs. Extra:** Concise, Authentic and Refined apply to all copy. **Inspiring** is the extra sparkle added to special moments — brand communication, emotional headlines, editorial content. Don't force it onto functional UI.

---

## 1. Concise — *Fundamental*

Write to be understood. Show confidence in clarity.

- **Clear** — Prioritize comprehension and use jargon sparingly.
- **Focused** — Get straight to the point, no meandering. One thought follows another.
- **Assured** — Avoid hesitant or ambivalent phrasing. Trust the facts (and the cars) to speak for themselves.

**Tips & tricks**
- Keep (most) sentences short.
- Use specific language or examples.
- Cut vague language and filler words: *very, indeed, really, kind of, essentially, in other words, it is possible that, needless to say, due to the fact that, in order to…*
- Beware of repeating yourself — but use important terms consistently.
- Use the right amount of information: too little leaves questions, too much obscures your purpose.

| ❌ Before | ✅ After |
|---|---|
| The Porsche 911 Carrera T is about concentrating on the essentials. The focus on agility, lightweight construction and an emotive soundscape, as well as its six-speed manual transmission, make this the ideal Carrera for purists. With agility, lightweight design, and a thrilling sound, its six-speed manual transmission makes it the purist's choice. Tailor-made equipment packages add a touch of personal style. For the first time, the Carrera T is also available as a cabriolet. |

## 2. Authentic — *Fundamental*

Speak like a person, not a corporation. Connect to your audience on eye level.

- **Genuine** — Refrain from exaggeration and overpromises. Avoid clichés.
- **Human** — Speak in active voice with a warm, approachable tone.
- **Personal** — Address people directly. Use "we" and "you" wherever possible.

**Tips & tricks — think about your audience**
- Who are you writing for?
- What do (and don't) they care about?
- What's most important to them?
- How can you show that you're writing *for them*?
- How do you want them to feel about Porsche?

**Tips & tricks — use inclusive language**
- Do not discredit any gender, ethnicity, religion, politics, or other value system.
- Speak to everyone, not just men (e.g. *humankind* instead of *mankind*, *spouse* instead of *husband*).
- Be respectful and act with empathy.
- Communicate on eye level — do not lecture or overexplain.

**Active voice & perspective**

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| You have successfully logged out. | Your log out has been successful. |
| Add your Porsche and manage your Connect services. | You have not registered a vehicle at the moment and can therefore only use My Porsche functions to a limited extent. |
| Unsere Highlights | Highlights |

**Rule:** If you can ask "by whom?" at the end of a sentence, it's passive — rewrite it. Brand-owned content uses "we/our"; user-owned content uses "you/your". Never write as if the brand is a distant third party.

**Before → After** *(Panamera)*

| ❌ Before | ✅ After |
|---|---|
| The Panamera is automotive design taken to the next level. The combination of Porsche personality and sedan practicality are truly remarkable. Unlike anything else on the road, this is a car that will literally stop traffic. | The Panamera combines classic sports car styling and performance with the practicality of a four-door sedan. The result? A car that never lets you forget you're driving a Porsche, even if you're only going to the office. |

## 3. Refined — *Fundamental*

Combine finesse and intelligence. Articulate an unmistakable attitude.

- **Polished** — Always choose words with care. Compose sentences logically and with uncompromising quality.
- **Flowing** — Be aware of rhythm, stops and starts. Avoid awkward phrasing.
- **Timeless** — Steer clear of slang or trendy phrases.
- **Humorous** — Don't forget your sense of humour, the twinkle in the eye. Always intelligent, sometimes confidently self-deprecating.

**Tips & tricks — one word can make all the difference**

Refined language often comes down to choosing one word over another. Selectively inserting a less common word raises copy above the everyday. Do it too often, though, and you start to sound pretentious.

| Instead of… | Use |
|---|---|
| remind | evoke |
| make | manufacture |
| consist | comprise |
| nice | outstanding |
| fun | pleasure |
| typical | characteristic |
| unique | singular |

**Don't say *iconic*. Don't say *luxurious*.**
Yes, Porsche is an iconic brand — but calling ourselves an icon isn't our style. Think about what Porsche stands for; use "renowned" or "symbol" instead. The same goes for "luxury": convince through quality, and let customers decide if we're luxury. No need to say it out loud.

**Correct & consistent writing** *(German specifics)*
- Hyphens in composites are mandatory: `Kurzarm-T-Shirt`, `Sportwagen-Enthusiasten`.
- "Porsche" may stand alone but does **not** legitimize dropping hyphens in all composites.
- Punctuation is used carefully — especially in headlines and CTAs: less is more.

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Dieses Porsche Kurzarm-T-Shirt ist das perfekte Kleidungsstück für alle Sportwagen-Enthusiasten. | Stylisches Porsche Kurzarm-T-Shirt mit Print der Weissach Tesstrecke und -Koordinaten. |

At Porsche Financial Services, we are here to make that dream a reality. ## 4. Inspiring — *Extra*

Paint pictures in people's minds. Make dreams become reality. **Use for special moments only** — brand communication, emotional headlines, editorial content. Not for everyday functional copy.

- **Vivid** — Be imaginative. Add concrete details that appeal to the senses.
- **Flowing** — Show your passion, express genuine enthusiasm.
- **Engaging** — Employ elements of storytelling.

**Tips & tricks — hints for better storytelling**
- **Develop a clear message.** Define the main idea or theme. Be specific, not generic. Use insights and facts as sources, garnish with anecdotes.
- **Focus on purpose and stakes.** Give people a reason to care. Why tell this story? How does it affect the audience? Which pain point are we solving?
- **Follow a narrative arc.** Give the copy shape — a beginning, middle and ending. Don't lose the thread.
- **Capture attention and interest.** Draw people in by surprising or intriguing them. Use captivating headlines and attention-grabbing subheads. Reinforce attention throughout.
- **Appeal to the senses.** Make it visual. Describe how things look, feel, sound and smell — help the audience "feel" the story.

Dream-related and automotive metaphors bring the brand to life — when they fit naturally. Never stretch them.

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Gestalten Sie Ihren Traum 911 Carrera | 911 gestalten |
| Nice to have you on board. | Thank you for registering with My Porsche. |
| We must have taken a wrong turn. Let's get you back on track. | Page not found. Please click here to return to Home. |

**Before → After** *(model descriptors)*

| ❌ Before (plain) | ✅ After (inspiring) |
|---|---|
| The mid-engine sports car for two made for pure driving pleasure | Seating for two, adrenaline for twenty. Mid-engine driving pleasure. |
| The sports car sedan for an active lifestyle with highest comfort | Living room comfort, racetrack excitement. Packed in a sedan. |

## Quick Copy Checklist

Before finalizing any copy:

- [ ] **Concise** — Short sentences, no filler, straight to the point?
- [ ] **Authentic** — Active voice, "we/you", warm and on eye level? No "has been", "was done"?
- [ ] **Refined** — Words chosen with care, good rhythm, no slang? Avoided "iconic"/"luxurious"? German composites hyphenated?
- [ ] **Inspiring** *(special moments only)* — Vivid, sensory, a story worth following?
- [ ] Specialized terms explained on first use?
- [ ] Would someone immediately recognize this as Porsche?

## Communication Types

A different tone for different communication types. Like a real person, we adjust our tone for each type of communication. These four clusters help adapt the ToV principles to our most common types of communication:

| # | Type | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Product Communication** | Inspirational product marketing and advertising |
| 2 | **Product Information** | Informational content around our products and services |
| 3 | **Brand Communication** | All types of marketing communication on brand level |
| 4 | **Corporate Communication** | Press Releases, Investor Relations, CSR, etc. |

> Use these as a **guide, not a strict rulebook**. Each type weights the four principles differently (●●●● = leading, ○ = minimal) and gives specific guidance per principle.

### 1. Product Communication

*Inspirational product marketing and advertising.*

| Principle | Emphasis | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Concise** | ●●●○ | Translate the technical into the easy to follow. Never compare with others — we are our own benchmark. Cut the marketing blah blah. |
| **Authentic** | ●●○○ | Use simple, everyday words rather than jargon or difficult terminology. Focus on what's essential. Don't overexplain. |
| **Refined** | ●●○○ | Explain complicated topics using clear analogies or examples. Understand and consider individual needs and challenges of your audience. |
| **Inspiring** | ●●●● | Instead of listing product features, explain their benefits for the customer. Don't just talk about passion — write passionately. |

### 2. Product Information

*Informational content around our products and services.*

| Principle | Emphasis | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Concise** | ●●●○ | Use typical Porsche terminology with pride, but clearly explain and define it to avoid gatekeeping. Switch to a more factual tone when appropriate. |
| **Authentic** | ●●●● | Use correct terminology. Be precise. Write useful, clear, easy to understand. |
| **Refined** | ●●●○ | Reflect the customers' world, use customer-focused language. Create personal interactions, not cold transactions. No hard selling. |
| **Inspiring** | ●○○○ | Don't just focus on the WHAT, think about the WHY and HOW. Always deliver value to the customer. Consider what's relevant to them. |

### 3. Brand Communication

*All types of marketing communication on brand level.*

| Principle | Emphasis | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Concise** | ●●●○ | Use strong verbs to amplify our discerning attitude. Leave distinctive Porsche terms in German (e.g. Werk). Smartly integrate wit and humor into your communication. |
| **Authentic** | ●●○○ | Be confident and bold, but never brag. Avoid arrogance. Use precise terms and substantiate statements with facts to underline credibility. |
| **Refined** | ●●○○ | Find ways to bring the customer's world into the story we're telling. Engage in a dialogue — ask questions, give answers. |
| **Inspiring** | ●●●● | Use metaphors to help readers make meaningful connections. Use automotive language judiciously to add color and interest to your writing. |

### 4. Corporate Communication

*Press Releases, Investor Relations, CSR, etc.*

| Principle | Emphasis | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Concise** | ●●●● | Place attributes only to enhance the quality of a statement, not to fill space. |
| **Authentic** | ●●●○ | Use short, crisp words, minimal adjectives and adverbs, and avoid jargon where possible. Prioritize — focus on the most important message only. |
| **Refined** | ●●○○ | Turn abstract ideas/concepts into tangible ones. Follow official language policy and the glossary, without sounding mechanical. |
| **Inspiring** | ●○○○ | Capture attention with a key insight. Wrap facts in inspiring copy, rather than just reporting. Keep the recipient in mind. |

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Leading principle |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | Bold, short, evocative — metaphor welcome | Inspiring |
| CTA | Active verb-first, max 4 words — "Jetzt entdecken", "Modell wählen" | Concise |
| Error state | Human, automotive metaphor where natural — never technical, never blaming | Authentic + Inspiring |
| Onboarding | Welcoming, "on board" — community language | Authentic |
| Confirmation | Direct, positive, active — "You've successfully…" | Concise + Authentic |
| Empty state | Inviting, not apologetic — point toward the next action | Authentic |
| Legal/Technical | Clear and simple — explain terms, no jargon dumps | Concise |
| Editorial / Brand story | Sensory, narrative, passionate | Inspiring |
