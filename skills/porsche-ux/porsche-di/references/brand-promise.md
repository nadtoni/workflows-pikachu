# Brand Promise: The Fastest Way into Porsche

The foundation for every digital product and customer journey. The primary evaluation criterion for any digital output.

This is our promise to Porsche fans and customers in the digital space. Ultimately, Porsche is about performance, driving, human experiences and meeting people. Our digital experience exists to enhance and provide access to that world — that's where the promise comes from.

---

## The Two Dimensions

### The Fastest Way → Performance

We translate the performance of Porsche cars into digital experience.

- Interactions are **quick, simple, focused, effortless**
- Time matters — no gimmicks
- Everything adapts to the context and need of the user
- Leverage newest technology to improve UX with **precision**
- Intuitive interfaces are **non-negotiable, everywhere**
- Space dedicated to **beautiful details** — as in the cars

**Attributes:** Simple · Contextual · Intuitive · Seamless · Focused · Purposeful · Intelligent · Effortless

### Into Porsche → Community

Porsche has one of the largest communities in the world. For many dreamers, digital is the **first direct contact point with the brand**.

- Interactions need to be **welcoming, personal and joyful**
- Access points to the world of Porsche are **easy and inviting**
- Communication is **simple, friendly and open**
- We build **connections and loyalty** in the digital space

**Attributes:** Welcoming · Inspirational · Design · Driving Fun · Personal · Exclusive · Heritage · Joyful

---

## Using the Promise as an Evaluator

Before shipping any digital output, ask:

1. Is this the **fastest** path to what the user needs?
2. Does this feel **effortlessly clear**, or does it require effort?
3. Does this feel **welcoming** to someone discovering Porsche for the first time?
4. Is there a **detail** here that reflects the craft and performance of the brand?
5. Does this **invite** the user deeper into the Porsche world, or does it close off?

If the answer to any of these is "no" or "I'm not sure" — that's where to focus.

---

## Who This Applies To

Porsche DI is not just for designers. The brand promise must be lived by everyone working on digital experiences:

- Product owners
- Project managers
- UX and UI designers
- Developers
- Content specialists and strategists

The promise is the shared language across all disciplines.
