# Design Codes

Five principles that define the visual and experiential qualities of Porsche DI. Each code spans all four layers of digital design, connecting them into one coherent brand language:

- **UX Design** — *Concept* (the underlying idea and structure) and *Flow* (how users move through the experience)
- **UI Design** — *Interface* (the visible, interactive surface) and *Content* (copy, media and information)

Porsche DI is not just visual — it shapes how we design digital experiences end to end. The codes must be lived by everyone on the team: product owners, project managers, UX and UI designers, developers, content specialists and strategists.

---

## 1. Stage the Legend

> Design every interface around a clear protagonist — cars, people, brand — and reduce everything that competes with it.

Focus on Porsche's rich heritage and outstanding products by giving them the strongest possible stage. Minimal distractions, clear focal point. What we show should always be presented with clarity and intention — every feature, content piece and interaction contributing to the same message.

**Key points**
- Create hero moments that elevate Porsche's icons: cars, people, products, stories
- Place the hero at the center — visually and conceptually
- Remove distractions to give the hero clarity and space
- Showcase details through full views, dynamic angles, crafted close-ups
- Make the user feel they are in the presence of something singular, timeless, important

**In practice**
- ✅ Full-bleed car imagery with minimal UI overlay
- ✅ One clear CTA, not three competing ones
- ✅ White space as a frame, not a void to fill
- ❌ Multiple competing focal points on one screen
- ❌ UI chrome that draws attention away from the product

---

## 2. Tell a Story

> Create journeys people can move through — starting with curiosity, leading into complexity and emotional narratives.

Go beyond delivery of information. Evoke emotion, lead users intuitively, shape interactions that have a beginning and an end. When the flow becomes a story people follow effortlessly, it becomes one they remember.

**Key points**
- Every interaction moves from clear overviews to deeper layers of detail
- Users control how far they explore — curiosity rewarded through purposeful clarity
- Complexity is not hidden but revealed with elegance and intent
- Start with concise entry points that invite deeper engagement

**In practice**
- ✅ Progressive disclosure: headline → summary → detail
- ✅ Transitions that carry meaning, not just decoration
- ✅ Content that earns the next scroll
- ❌ Dumping all information at once
- ❌ Dead ends — every screen should invite the next step

---

## 3. Boost Interaction

> Make every interaction feel sharp, direct and rewarding — with immediate feedback, precise behavior and space for playful exploration.

Interfaces should feel vivid, adaptive and responsive. They offer multiple entry points and incorporate ambient or conversational elements where needed. The goal: encourage discovery, provide control, reflect the precision and excitement of driving a Porsche.

**Key points**
- Interfaces feel vivid, responsive and adaptive, shaped around the individual
- Interaction balances emotional engagement with engineering precision — depth without overload
- Evolving layouts and responsive widget-inspired elements express a brand in motion
- Environments adapt and invite exploration through customization, responsiveness, transitions

**In practice**
- ✅ Immediate, satisfying micro-feedback on every interaction
- ✅ Dynamic cards and widgets that respond to context
- ✅ Ambient content (video loops, motion) that adds life without noise
- ❌ Sluggish transitions or delayed feedback
- ❌ Static layouts that feel inert

---

## 4. Keep the Focus

> Design with confidence and intent — stay focused on what matters, avoid unnecessary noise, complexity or distraction.

Establish a clear rhythm and flow by balancing dynamic interactive moments with calm and simple phases. Confident choices reduce elements to the essential. This intentional clarity creates tension and precision reflecting Porsche's performance-driven spirit.

**Key points**
- Calm moments create pause and breathing space, establishing rhythm and contrast
- Balance density with simplicity — performance with pause
- Every element is intentional: appears only when needed, never more and never less
- Use space as a design tool to heighten focus and create flow
- Alternate speed with pause, complexity with simplicity — effortless and exacting

**In practice**
- ✅ One primary action per screen
- ✅ Generous negative space between sections
- ✅ Typography that creates hierarchy through contrast, not decoration
- ❌ Feature creep — adding elements "just in case"
- ❌ Filling empty space with content that has no purpose

---

## 5. Tailor Moments

> Build strong systems, but leave room to deliberately break uniformity and personalize — small unexpected details that give the brand character, tension and memorability.

Interfaces are adaptable through theming that aligns with communication goals and reflects personal preferences. Combining personalization with contextual content creates relevant, meaningful moments.

**Key points**
- Themes shape the mood: personal, expressive, connected
- They go beyond color — extending to typography, motion, sound, materials, iconography
- Themes adapt fluidly: curated by brand, personalized by user, or responsive to real-world context (driving mode, car color, preference)
- Personalization delivers the most relevant content at the right time and place
- **The standard for Porsche DI is always neutral — light and dark. Colorful themes only for special purposes** (product launch, customization)

**In practice**
- ✅ A detail that surprises — an animation, a sound, a contextual message
- ✅ Dark/light mode as baseline, always
- ✅ Themed moments tied to specific product or launch contexts
- ❌ Colorful themes as default UI
- ❌ Personalization that feels intrusive rather than considered
